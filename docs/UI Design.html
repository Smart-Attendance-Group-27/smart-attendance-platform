Microinteractions
• Course-card swipe — 150–220ms ease-out momentum scroll, pagination dot slides
• Tab transition — 180ms cross-fade + 4px slide between Sessions / Attendance
• Active-session countdown — Numeral ticks down live, no animation on the container
• Face-verification progress — Ring border fills clockwise around the oval guide
• QR detection — Corner guides pulse subtly on lock-on, then snap solid
• Geofence checking — Status row shows a 250ms indeterminate progress dash
• Successful attendance — Checkmark draws in over 300ms, then settles — no confetti
• Pull-to-refresh — Standard platform spinner, 220ms release ease
• Bottom-sheet opening — 280ms spring slide-up with scrim fade
• Dark-mode transition — 150ms cross-fade of background/text tokens
Accessibility
  • Minimum 4.5:1 text contrast in both light and dark mode
  • 48×48dp minimum touch targets throughout
  • Every status pairs an icon, a text label, and an accessible colour — never colour alone
  • Meaningful screen-reader labels on every icon-only control
  • Layouts hold together under dynamic text scaling
  • Visible focus and pressed states on all interactive elements
  • Critical text never sits directly on busy photography without a scrim
Content & terminology
Use
Attendance marked · Identity verified · QR verified · Classroom location verified · Active · Upcoming · Missed · Closed · Verification failed
Avoid
“Checked in” right after face verification · “Overdue” · “Face recognized” · “Tracking you” · “Biometric scan complete” · “Course tab” for the sessions tab
