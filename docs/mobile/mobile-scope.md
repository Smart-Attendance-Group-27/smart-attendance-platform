The mobile application is used by students only.

Lecturers and administrators use the web dashboard.

## Included Functions

- Student authentication
- Mandatory password change
- Consent and permission introduction
- Trial face verification
- Student home dashboard
- Weekly timetable
- Course and lecture information
- Start-of-lecture attendance check-in
- Geofence validation
- Liveness and face capture
- Attendance result
- Optional lecturer-controlled dynamic QR verification
- Attendance history
- Notifications
- Student profile
- Settings
- Help and troubleshooting

## Verification Workflow

At the beginning of a lecture, the student completes geofence validation, liveness checking, and face verification within the configured check-in window.

Dynamic QR verification is optional. A lecturer may activate QR verification separately during a lecture.
